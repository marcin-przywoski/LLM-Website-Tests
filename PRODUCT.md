# DevOps Engineer Resume

## Register
Brand (portfolio)

## Users
- Hiring managers and recruiters evaluating DevOps candidates
- Technical leads assessing infrastructure and deployment expertise
- CTO/Engineering managers reviewing system reliability and automation skills

## Product Purpose
A professional portfolio resume that demonstrates DevOps expertise through:
- Technical proficiency with statistics and metrics
- Key achievements and project impact
- Industry certifications and continuous learning
- Modern, tech-forward visual presentation that reflects DevOps culture

## Brand Voice
- Professional yet approachable
- Technically credible without jargon overload
- Confident in expertise
- Modern and forward-thinking

## Tone
- Clear and direct
- Data-driven (metrics-focused)
- Engaging but not casual
- Emphasizes reliability, automation, and efficiency

## Anti-references
- Dated resume templates (WordArt, clip art, 2000s styling)
- Overly generic "marketing" language
- Cluttered timelines or lists
- Lack of visual hierarchy
- Inaccessible color contrast or tiny text

## Design Direction
Modern tech-forward aesthetic inspired by infrastructure dashboards and DevOps culture. Clean, sophisticated typography with strategic color accents. Emphasis on data visualization for achievements and metrics. Smooth interactions and transitions that feel responsive and polished.

## Strategic Principles
1. **Impact-first**: Lead with metrics and business outcomes, not just tasks
2. **Credibility**: Back claims with certifications, technologies, and proven results
3. **Scanability**: Support both deep reading and quick scanning
4. **Modern tech aesthetic**: Dark-friendly, dashboard-inspired design language
5. **Responsiveness**: Beautiful on all devices (print, mobile, desktop, tablet)
